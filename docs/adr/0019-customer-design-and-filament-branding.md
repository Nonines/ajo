# ADR 0019: Customer design and Filament branding

Status: Accepted; concrete visual tokens await design sign-off.

## Decision

- Apply playful neo-brutalism, editorial minimalism, and an indie-creator personality across the existing public homepage, authentication, and customer application.
- Use bold editorial typography, generous whitespace, strong grids, hard borders, offset shadows, flat colour accents, and selective zine/grunge details. References include Swiss graphic design, indie zines, modern SaaS, Gumroad, and Windscribe.
- Keep financial amounts, statuses, and confirmations clear. Layouts must not invent products, rates, testimonials, adoption figures, or regulatory guarantees.
- Establish the foundation in phase 0 and extend it through subsequent financial features.
- Brand Filament through colours, typography, and logo, retaining native layouts and controls. Keep Filament and Svelte component implementations separate.
- Obtain design sign-off on palette, fonts, textures, logo treatment, and related tokens before UI implementation. Preserve appearance behavior and verify shared-layout/component consumers.

## Rationale

The direction gives customer surfaces a bold, approachable identity while keeping financial information legible. Restrained Filament branding maintains coherence with its native interface.

## Consequences

Token values remain intentionally unselected. References guide the direction without authorizing copied assets. This scope covers existing application surfaces, not a separate marketing product or later demo-polish phase.
